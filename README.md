---

## ✦ PROJECT OVERVIEW ✦

A&S Tires is a responsive business website designed for a wholesale tire sales and delivery company.

The website gives customers one central location where they can:

- Learn about wholesale tire services
- Understand who the business serves
- Review delivery coverage
- Learn about ordering requirements
- Review deposit and cancellation policies
- Request pricing information
- Contact the business

The project demonstrates how a service-based wholesale business can move from phone calls and word-of-mouth referrals toward a more organized online presence.

---

## ✦ THE BUSINESS PROBLEM ✦

A&S Tires primarily relied on direct conversations and word-of-mouth referrals to explain its services, delivery area, and ordering process.

Potential customers needed a centralized location where they could quickly determine:

- Whether A&S Tires offers the service they need
- Whether delivery is available in their state
- How wholesale ordering works
- Whether a deposit may be required
- How cancellation and refund policies work
- How to request pricing
- How to contact the business

Without a dedicated website, the business could receive repeated questions before a customer was ready to discuss an order.

---

## ✦ THE SOLUTION ✦

I designed and developed a responsive website that serves as an informational and customer-inquiry hub for A&S Tires.

The website provides:

- Wholesale tire-service information
- Multi-state delivery coverage
- Ordering and deposit policies
- Cancellation information
- Pricing-inquiry guidance
- Direct contact options
- Responsive desktop and mobile layouts
- Professional business branding

The experience is organized around the questions a potential wholesale customer may need answered before contacting the business.

---

## ✦ CUSTOMER JOURNEY ✦

The primary customer journey follows these steps:

1. **Discover the business**  
   The customer is introduced to A&S Tires and its wholesale services.

2. **Understand the services**  
   The customer learns what the business provides and who it serves.

3. **Review delivery coverage**  
   The customer checks whether service may be available in their state.

4. **Understand ordering policies**  
   The customer reviews deposit, cancellation, and ordering expectations.

5. **Request pricing information**  
   The customer uses the available contact pathway to discuss tire availability and pricing.

6. **Confirm details directly**  
   The business confirms current inventory, pricing, delivery availability, deposits, and order requirements.

---

## ✦ DESIGN DECISIONS ✦

### Service Information Comes First

Wholesale services are introduced early so visitors can quickly determine whether the business may fit their needs.

### Visible Delivery Coverage

The delivery area is displayed clearly because location is one of the customer's most important questions.

### Policies Before Contact

Deposit and cancellation policies are presented before the customer begins an order discussion. This helps establish expectations earlier in the experience.

### Direct Contact Path

The website provides clear contact options instead of requiring customers to search through unrelated content.

### Professional Visual Identity

The layout, imagery, typography, and colors create a business-focused presentation appropriate for a wholesale and delivery company.

### Mobile-Friendly Design

Customers may access the website while working from a shop, warehouse, vehicle, or mobile device. The responsive layout keeps service and contact information available on smaller screens.

## ✦ ORDER AND DEPOSIT INFORMATION ✦

Wholesale orders may require a deposit at the time of order.

As represented on the website:

- Deposits may be refundable when an order is canceled within 24 hours.
- After 24 hours, deposits may become nonrefundable due to restocking, labor, and preparation time.

Customers should confirm the current deposit, cancellation, refund, delivery, and ordering policies directly with A&S Tires before placing an order.

The website does not independently finalize orders or process payments.
